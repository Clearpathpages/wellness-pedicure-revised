Privacy

Your privacy, handled with care.

How Wellness Pedicures collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who we are

Wellness Pedicures is a medical, waterless pedicure studio at 5400 S University Dr, Ste 402, Davie, FL 33328. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean Wellness Pedicures. You can reach us at wellnesspedicuresbyjay@gmail.com or (954) 594-4771.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • New client assessment: your name, email, phone number, the service you're interested in, the foot or nail concerns you select, any medical considerations you choose to share (such as diabetes or circulation issues), preferred days and times, notes, and up to four photos of your feet or nails.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, and message.
  • Phone, text, and email: whatever you share when you contact us directly.

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To review your assessment and recommend the right service and pricing before your first visit.
  • To reply to your questions and contact you about your appointment.
  • To provide safe care that takes your health considerations into account.

We never sell your information, and we never share it with anyone for marketing.

Who can see it

Your assessment details and photos are stored privately and are seen only by the Wellness Pedicures team, including our medical nail technicians.

We rely on a small number of service providers to run this website and our bookings:

  • Base44 stores assessment submissions and photos for us. Photos are kept in private storage and are not publicly accessible.
  • Square handles online booking, deposits, and payments. When you book, Square processes your details under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Google Fonts supplies its typefaces. Like most web services, they may log basic technical information such as your IP address when pages load.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep assessment details and photos for as long as you're a client, or as long as we need them to plan and record your care. If you don't become a client, we delete your assessment on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information you've sent us at any time. Email wellnesspedicuresbyjay@gmail.com or call (954) 594-4771, and we'll respond promptly.

Sending photos and health details is optional. If you'd rather not use the online form, you can discuss your feet with us in person at a consultation.

Keeping it safe

We use secure (HTTPS) connections and private storage, and we limit access to the people who need it to care for you. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid putting sensitive medical details in the general contact form; the new client assessment is the right place for those.

Children

This website is meant for adults. If you're booking for someone under 18, a parent or guardian should complete the assessment.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
